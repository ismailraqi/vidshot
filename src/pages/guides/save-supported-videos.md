Saving a video starts with a supported source and permission to download it. VidShot brings browsing and downloading into one app, so you can go from finding a video to keeping a copy in your library.

## Before you begin

You need an internet connection to browse and download. Make sure your device has room for the video, and only save content you own or have permission to download. A video being playable on a website does not mean it is available to save.

VidShot does not promise support for every website or every video. Download availability and quality options depend on the source.

## 1. Open the video in VidShot

Use the built-in browser to visit your video’s source and open its page. When VidShot offers a download for a supported video, use the download control to open the download sheet.

If no download option appears, the source or video may not be supported. You can also check your connection and confirm that the page has loaded. VidShot is not a way to bypass a source’s download restrictions.

## 2. Choose quality and a destination

The download sheet shows the available video quality choices. Pick the option that suits how you want to watch and the storage space you have available. Higher quality generally means a larger file.

Choose a destination using the **Save to** field. Keeping related videos in a folder from the start makes them easier to find later. See the [organization guide](/guides/organize-downloads/) for ideas.

## 3. Download and check the saved video

Start the download and let it finish before disconnecting. Open **Downloads** and find the saved video. Play it once to check that it is the video you intended to save.

Now you can [prepare for offline viewing](/guides/watch-offline/). Browsing for new videos and downloading additional files will still need an internet connection.

## If the download does not work

- Confirm that your internet connection is working.
- Check that your device has enough free storage.
- Reopen the source page and look for an available download option.
- Try an available lower quality if file size is a concern.
