A saved video is ready for the moments when streaming is inconvenient: a journey, a quiet break, or a place without a reliable connection. The key is to finish downloading before you go offline.

## Get ready while you are connected

First, [save a supported video](/guides/save-supported-videos/) in VidShot. Wait for the download to finish, then find it in **Downloads**. Play it briefly to check that the file opens correctly.

For a useful final check, disconnect from Wi-Fi and mobile data and open the saved file again. Do this before you leave, so you have time to resolve an incomplete download.

## Open your video from Downloads

Use the copy in your Downloads library when you want to watch offline. Opening the original website in the browser still needs a connection; your saved file is the one to play.

If you cannot find a video, check the folder you selected when downloading. Clear names and a small set of folders help—see [how to organize your downloads](/guides/organize-downloads/).

## Make playback fit your moment

VidShot includes several ways to enjoy saved videos:

- **Picture in Picture:** keep the video in a floating player while you use another app.
- **Background play:** keep playback going in the background or while your screen is locked.
- **Playback speed:** choose a speed that suits what you are watching.
- **Sleep timer:** set a timer when you want playback to stop after a while.

You can use the app on iPhone or iPad. Save the videos you want on the device you plan to use; these guides do not assume that downloads automatically sync between devices.

## If a video will not play offline

Reconnect and check whether the download finished. Make sure you are opening the saved file rather than the source page. If the file still will not open, return to the supported source and try downloading it again while connected.

Keep enough free space for the files you want to take with you. When you no longer need a video, review it in Downloads before deleting it.
