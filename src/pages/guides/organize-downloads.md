A useful video library is one you can find your way around. VidShot includes folders and file actions to help you keep your saved collection manageable on iPhone and iPad.

## Start with a few useful folders

Choose categories that match how you watch. A **Travel** folder could hold videos for a journey, while **Watch later** could collect things you have not played yet. These are examples of folder names, not automatic categories.

Use folders in **Downloads** to group related files. When [saving a new video](/guides/save-supported-videos/), choose its destination in the download sheet’s **Save to** field so it arrives in the right place.

## Give files names you recognize

A source’s file name might be difficult to remember. Open a video’s file actions and choose **Rename** to give it a clearer name. Use a short description that helps you identify the content later.

For example, a name such as “Weekend walking route” tells you more than a long sequence of letters and numbers. Keep names distinct if you save several similar videos.

## Move videos as your collection changes

Use **Move to** in a file’s actions to place it in the folder you want. Review your **Watch later** folder occasionally and move the videos you want to keep into longer-term collections.

The file actions also show options for sharing and saving to Photos or Files. Check the action you select so the video goes to the destination you intended.

## Keep private videos in Secret Vault

VidShot’s **Secret Vault** keeps private videos behind a passcode. Use the **Lock in Secret Vault** action for a video you want to keep there.

Keep your passcode in a safe place. A passcode-protected view is useful for privacy, but this guide does not claim encryption, backup, or a particular recovery process. Those details need to come from the final app documentation and [privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Use the right tool for the job

VidShot also includes video trimming, editing, audio, and compression tools. Trimming and advanced editing, audio, and compression tools require **Premium**. Check the purchase information in the app at launch before choosing a paid feature.

Before removing a file, make sure you no longer need that copy. Good organization also means leaving enough storage for your next download.
